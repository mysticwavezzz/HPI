Methodology. Net approval = approve % − disapprove %. Margin of error is computed at 95% confidence from each survey’s sample size (n) as ±1.96 × √(0.25 / n), the conservative p = 0.5 case; pooled weekly/monthly points use their combined n. Status badges compare the latest net rating with the previous poll cycle: ▲ Rising (+3.0 or more), ▼ Falling (−3.0 or more), ▬ Steady (within ±1.0), △/▽ Edging (between 1.0 and 3.0, shown neutral), and Stale when no poll in 90 days.
